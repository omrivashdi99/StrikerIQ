Player Development

The Attacking Toolkit

The skills behind every good attacking decision, on and off the ball.

Scanning

Checking your surroundings before the ball arrives so you already know your options the moment you receive it.

First Touch

Your first touch sets up everything after it — a good one buys time, space, and options; a bad one gives them away.

Body Orientation

Facing your body toward the field, not the ball, so you can play forward, turn, or shield without wasted movement.

Timing of Runs

Arriving in space a half-second after the defense expects you to, not before — timing beats raw speed.

1v1 Decision-Making

Reading what a defender is inviting you to do, then choosing the option they're least prepared for.

Finishing

Picking the target the goalkeeper is least likely to reach, and committing to the strike without hesitation.

Creating Space

Moving to lose your marker just before the ball arrives — a change of pace or direction, not standing still.

About

About Striker IQ

Striker IQ is an educational concept built to help attacking soccer players — wingers and strikers in particular — practice the decision-making side of the game, not just the physical side. Technical ability and fitness matter, but games are also won and lost in the half-second choices players make with the ball: when to pass, when to dribble, when to shoot, and when to hold.

This site presents realistic attacking scenarios and common tactical principles so players can think through those moments away from the pressure of a live match, and build better instincts for when it counts.

Striker IQ is an educational training concept created for a class project. It is not a scientifically validated tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by any professional soccer club, coaching body, or sports science organization. It is intended purely as a decision-making learning aid.
